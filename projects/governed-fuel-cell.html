Governed Fuel Cell Project

🛠

A fuel cell system requires isothermal, steady-flow conditions in order to operate. This project is an attempt to simulate on maintaining these conditions through real-time temperature and humidity monitoring. The system actively manages enthalpy (heat rejection) and entropy (through byproduct drainage) to prevent flooding or overheating within the cell housing.

The ESP32 serves as the central hub, utilizing I2C communication via SDA (Pin 21) and SCL (Pin 22). Thermo and Gyro modules are daisy-chained through QWIIC connectors. The system's physical response is handled through GPIO 13 driving the enthalpy-management fan, while GPIO 18 and GPIO 19 actuate the entropy-regulating servos for the valve and vent systems.

The Logic & Calculation Framework

\[ \Delta H = Q - W_e \] \[ \Delta H + W_e = Q \] \[ W_e = nFE \] \[ \Delta H + nFE = Q \]

If T > 25 C, motor fan ON.

(Fan) PIN 13 = Qcalc

Since Q is a unit of energy, this Qcalc is then changed to a PWM value for the ESP32 by:

\[ PWM_{Signal} = k \cdot (T_{measured} - T_{target}) \]

Enthalpy Management: Isothermal Heat Rejection

To prevent the fuel cell from overheating, the system relates Gibbs Free Energy to the change in enthalpy. The ESP32 calculates the required heat rejection (Qcalc, derived from Q above) to maintain an isothermal state. This value is then mapped to the Fan (DC Motor) speed, which acts as a dynamic heat sink to exhaust excess thermal energy generated during the electrochemical reaction.

General Environmental Management

Entropy Management: Water Byproduct Removal

The overall chemical reaction of the PEMFC (H2 + 1/2O2 -> H2O) results in water condensate. If water builds up, it disrupts the steady-flow state and can "flood" the cathode.

The Logic: If the measured temperature T <= Tdp, the air is saturated and condensation is imminent.

The Action: The ESP32 triggers a Servo-controlled valve to open, venting the system and removing liquid water to maintain the entropy balance.

\[ \text{If DPS} \geq \text{T, open valve (PIN 18)} \]
